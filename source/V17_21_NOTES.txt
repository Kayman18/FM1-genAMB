genAMB v17.21 — pad audio crash workaround

User's v17.20 device report:
VEC 00000001, PC 0200cc76, EMU 00000000, DBG 00000010,
RET 0200fb78, DURING AUDIO RENDER.

The exact v17.20 binary contains an invalid pointer sequence in the inlined Pad 1
control/envelope update: r7 is cleared to zero and then [r7+16] is loaded.
The reported PC is immediately after that load. DBG bit 4 is a bus-invalid guard
flag. This points to bad generated code, not the splash artwork. RET points into
Rings modulation, which is the preceding call; it does not identify the faulting
load. A debug-enabled rebuild changed instruction addresses, so the diagnosis uses
the saved original v17.20 ELF, not addresses from the debug rebuild.

FIX
Prevent inlining of pad_coefs, pad2_coefs_lite, render_pad and render_pad2.
This reduces live-register pressure and preserves separate pad control/render
calls. Inspection of the new device code shows pad_coefs loading its pointer from
the argument and retaining it through the formerly failing envelope load.
The exact before/after instructions are included in tests/startup21.

Both pads, Rings, reverb, delay, LFO waveform display, octave/probability controls,
preset storage and migration remain. The v17.20 defined exponent arithmetic and
15-second labeled crash display remain. No presets are erased or relocated.
