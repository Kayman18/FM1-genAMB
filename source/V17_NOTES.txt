AMB-1 Full Rings - Clouds Clean Reverb v17

Distortion / bitcrush correction
--------------------------------
- Fixes the real overload path instead of masking it with saturation or a hard clip.
- Reverb tank input gain returns to the conservative 0.24 value.
- Reverb wet-return makeup is reduced to 1.10x at 100% instead of 1.25x.
- Master mix has about 3 dB more headroom than v16.
- Adds a smooth peak guard that is exactly linear below +/-0.90 and only acts on true overload.
- No always-on tanh saturation and no hard converter clamp in the normal signal path.
- 0% Reverb remains fully dry; 100% remains wet-only.
- Keeps 32-bit floating full-rate reverb storage and the long 10-20 s decay target.
- Slightly relaxes v16's very dark damping curve to avoid concentrating long-tail energy in a harsh mid band.

Unchanged
---------
- Enlarged info panel and vertical VU meters.
- Mixer/page mappings and all instrument controls.
- Full Rings integration and deferred model switching.
- Existing pad, bass, drone, percussion, and Rings reverb sends.
