AMB-1 Full Rings - Clouds Clean Reverb v16

Reverb / artifact fix
---------------------
- Keeps the v15 full-rate 44.1 kHz, 32-bit floating Clouds-style reverb tank.
- Removes the old 2.0x wet-return boost; wet return is now 1.25x.
- Raises tank input gain from 0.24 to 0.32 so the effect stays clearly audible without overdriving the master output.
- Makes Reverb a true 0-100% dry/wet control: 0% = dry only, 100% = wet only. v15 still leaked 10% dry at 100%, which could create metallic/phase colour with early reflections.
- Removes the always-active master tanh stage and uses transparent headroom with a final safety clamp only at converter limits.
- Restores Clouds' original 0.625 diffusion coefficient and darkens the feedback damping range to reduce glassy/metallic high-frequency ringing.
- Keeps the approximately 10-20 second tail target and full-rate modulation.

Unchanged
---------
- v15/v14 four-row UI, enlarged info panel and vertical VU meters.
- Mixer/page mappings and all instrument controls.
- Full Rings integration and deferred model switching.
- Pad, Drone, Bass, percussion and Rings reverb sends.
