Delay-mix changes only:
1. Dry Rings stays at unity for every non-zero delay Mix value.
2. Delay is now an additive wet return instead of a dry/wet crossfade.
3. Wet-return gain is 35% stronger at Mix 100 (1.35x instead of 1.0x).
4. Mix uses a square-root curve so the lower half of the knob is more useful:
     Mix 25 -> wet gain ~0.675x
     Mix 50 -> wet gain ~0.955x
     Mix 75 -> wet gain ~1.169x
     Mix 100 -> wet gain 1.350x
   At Mix 50, the delayed return is therefore about 1.91x the old 0.5x wet contribution (+5.6 dB),
   while the dry Rings signal remains full instead of being attenuated to 0.5x.
5. The nonlinear Mix curve is calculated only when the control changes and cached; no sqrt is performed per audio sample.
6. Feedback, Repeats, Time mapping, delay storage, damping and quantization behavior are unchanged.
7. Mix 0 remains a true bypass and retains the incremental buffer clear behavior.
